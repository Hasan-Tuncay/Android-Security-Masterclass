# OWASP Code of Conduct 🛡️

The **OWASP Android Security Masterclass** project is dedicated to providing a safe, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone. We strictly follow and enforce the **[OWASP Foundation Code of Conduct](https://owasp.org/www-policy/operational/code-of-conduct)**.

---

## 🤝 Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## 📜 Standards & Guidelines

### Expected Behavior
* Demonstrating empathy, professional courtesy, and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and technical experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the project and the broader cybersecurity community

### Unacceptable Behavior
* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, or political disparagement
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit permission
* Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source environment

---
